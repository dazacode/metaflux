# Code of conduct

## Scope

Issues, PRs, discussions and commit messages.

## Expectations

- Be specific. "It broke" is a starting point, not a report.
- Assume the other person read the docs you did not write.
- Credit upstreams and prior art.
- Accept that a provider can be unavailable through no fault of the project.
- Keep reviews about the code.

## Unacceptable

- harassment or personal attacks
- demanding free labour on a schedule
- shipping a required key and calling it keyless
- copying an adapter without its licence note

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close or revert. Persistent problems get a warning, then a block. Ask for a ruling in the issue; do not litigate in three threads.
